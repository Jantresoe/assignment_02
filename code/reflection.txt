That is why later on, I was unable to find the problem within my coding because I was mainly focused on a single page and ignoring how to connect it with my other modules. Thankfully, the integration test allowed me to see not which of my code was wrong but how I got it wrong, from the wording to even the wrong function in the public API. As I worked through my errors, I became more comfortable debugging and understanding the structure of the project. The assignment helped me understand different parts of the ETL process: extract, transform, and display, while also showing me that I should just focus on one module at a time, but instead look at how everything connects and how one error can affect everything so that I can do well in large Python projects.
